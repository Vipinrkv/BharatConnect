All feed posts, messages, user profiles, and conversations are stored locally on your device in a high-speed Room SQLite database. When you compose posts or send messages offline, they are queued instantly and synced seamlessly in the background via Android WorkManager as soon as network connectivity is restored.
Every direct conversation generates a local cryptographic key pair using AES-256 GCM. Messages are encrypted locally on the sender's device before transmission and can only be decrypted by the intended recipient's device. No plaintext is ever accessible to intermediate servers or third parties.
